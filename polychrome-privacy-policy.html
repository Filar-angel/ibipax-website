Polychrome: Restoration Simulator

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

In short: Polychrome collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the game sends nothing about you to us. Your progress stays on your device, and in your own backups or Steam Cloud if you use them. Purchases are handled by the App Store, Google Play or Steam.

1. Who we are

Ibi Pax Ltd. (“Ibi Pax”, “we”, “us”), a company incorporated in Hong Kong, makes and publishes Polychrome: Restoration Simulator (“Polychrome” or “the game”) for iOS, Android and PC on Steam. This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

It covers the game only. Our app Ibi Pax: Catholic Treasures has its own privacy policy. The rules for using the game are in the Polychrome terms of service.

2. Information we collect

None. Polychrome has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or any other personal details. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools. It does not connect to any server of ours, and it does not send information about you or the way you play to us or to anyone else.

3. What stays on your device

Your progress, your settings, the discoveries in your Artificer’s Journal and whether you have unlocked Chapters II, III and IV are stored on your device, inside the game. We cannot see them.

  • On iPhone, iPad and Android, the game also copies your progress to a save file in its own private storage on your device, so that it is not lost if your device clears the game’s web data to free up space. Like the rest of your apps’ data, this file can be included in your device’s own backup (iCloud, or your Google account’s backup) if you have backups turned on. That copy is held by Apple or Google under your account with them.
  • On PC, your progress is saved in a file on your computer, in your Windows user folder (AppData\Roaming\Polychrome). If Steam Cloud is turned on in your Steam settings, Steam keeps a copy of that file so that your progress can follow you to another computer. Valve holds that copy under its own privacy policy.
  • Erasing your progress: Settings, then Reset data, erases it at any time. Deleting the game removes what it keeps on your device; copies in your backups or in Steam Cloud are managed through your Apple, Google or Steam account.

4. The technical log on PC

The PC version keeps a small log file on your computer to help us answer support questions. It records the game’s version, your system, your screen and graphics card, any errors, the screens you opened and when the game closed. It keeps only your last two sessions.

It contains nothing personal: the name of your Windows user folder is left out, and nothing you make or save in the game is written to it. The log never leaves your computer unless you choose to send it to us; Settings, then Open log folder, shows where it is. If you send it, we use it only to answer your question. The iPhone, iPad and Android versions keep no log.

5. Purchases

Any purchase in Polychrome is made through the App Store (Apple), Google Play (Google) or Steam (Valve). They process your payment and your store account under their own privacy policies; we never receive your card or payment details.

The game keeps a record on your device that a purchase was made, and asks the store to confirm it when you restore a purchase.

6. Sharing and saving images

When you choose to share a restoration or save it, the game creates the image on your device and hands it to your device’s share sheet or photo library. On PC, it is saved to your Downloads folder. We do not receive it.

7. Links to other websites

A few screens link to outside sources, such as a museum’s page about an artwork. If you open one, it opens in your browser, outside the game, and that website’s own privacy policy applies.

8. Permissions and internet access

  • iPhone and iPad: Polychrome asks only for permission to add images to your photo library, the first time you save one. It cannot see the photos already there.
  • Android: Polychrome lists two permissions. Internet access is standard for apps built like Polychrome, and the game does not use it to send any data about you. Vibration is for the small taps you feel as you work.
  • Everywhere: the game never asks for your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

9. Children

Polychrome collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

10. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal data from the game, there is nothing about you for us to access, correct or delete. Information held by Apple, Google or Valve is managed through your account with them.

If you write to us, we use your message only to reply to you, and keep it only for as long as we need to.

11. Changes to this policy

If Polychrome changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if we add a new service, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the game.
