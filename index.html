“They were never grey.”

Beneath centuries of soot, smoke and yellowed varnish, Europe’s cathedrals once blazed with colour. In this relaxing, casual restoration game, travel from Chartres to Milan at your own pace, learn the crafts of real conservators, and bring twenty-one masterpieces back to light.

How it plays

Clean. Discover. Restore.

Every artwork in Polychrome is a real masterpiece in a real place. You work on it the way a conservator would: patiently, layer by layer, until the colour comes back. There’s no timer and no game over, so play for five minutes or a whole evening, and pick up where you left off.

I

Clean

Sweep soot from carved stone, wipe smoke from stained glass, roll away amber varnish. Soft brushes, silk cloths and cotton swabs, one patient stroke at a time.

II

Discover

As the surface clears, look closer. Saints, symbols and lost inscriptions wait to be found, and each one adds a page to the Artificer’s Journal: 168 stories of history and craft.

III

Restore

Then step up to the workbench. Lay and burnish gold leaf, rebuild shattered windows, carve and set new stone, mix El Greco’s crimson: a different craft for every masterpiece.

The pilgrimage

A road of colour across Europe

Follow the road on a hand-painted atlas, from the Royal Portal at Chartres to Leonardo’s Last Supper in Milan. Every chapter ends with its own film, and the pilgrimage with a finale of everything you have restored.
