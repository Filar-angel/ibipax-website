Our vision

Our commitment

10%

of our net revenue, given back

Each year, we give 10% of net revenue to help preserve heritage monuments and publish where it went. We support organisations that restore churches, chapels, sacred art and other holy places.

  • Sacred Architecture

    Restoring chapels, churches and cathedrals threatened by time and neglect.

  • Sacred Art

    Preserving frescoes, mosaics, sculptures and manuscripts for future generations.

  • Sacred Heritage

    Supporting the living transmission of chant, liturgy and spiritual tradition.

We publish a report on our giving at the end of every year. The same pledge runs through Polychrome, our restoration game.
